# Auto Accept Next Writ

Turning in a stack of master writs means opening each sealed writ from your bag, accepting it, crafting, turning in, and back to the bag. With this addon, as soon as you turn in a master writ, the next sealed writ you have **already crafted for** is opened and accepted for you.

A writ counts as crafted when WritWorthy marks it as completed, or when Dolgubon's Lazy Writ Crafter has crafted it (the checkmark in your bag).

## Commands

- `/ww`: opens WritWorthy with its materials list and queues every writ for crafting, in one go.
- `/aanwtest`: accepts the next crafted writ right now, without turning one in first.

## Requirements

- WritWorthy
- Optional: Dolgubon's Lazy Writ Crafter
