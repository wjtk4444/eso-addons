## Title: EvenBetterScoreboard
## APIVersion: 101044
## Version: 1.1

## Description: Battlegrounds scoreboard with damage/heal columns, class icons, a configurable name display mode, a multi-queue "EBS BG Queue" tab in the Group & Activity Finder, a requeue cooldown timer after declining (or missing) a ready check, and remembers your last selected mode on the built-in Battlegrounds queue screen. Optional LibAddonMenu-2.0 settings panel for name display, quick-queue-button visibility, and queue/scoreboard colors. Self-contained -- does not require Better Scoreboard, BetterBetterScoreboard, or BattlegroundModeSaver.
